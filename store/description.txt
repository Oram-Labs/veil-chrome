Use Claude and ChatGPT without handing over your private details.

Veil hides names, email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers and other sensitive values before claude.ai or chatgpt.com send your message. The AI service only sees placeholders. When the reply comes back, Veil puts the real values back in, so you read and copy the answer as if nothing had been hidden.

HOW IT WORKS
1. You write your message as usual.
2. Before the site sends it, the Veil app on your computer (or, without the app, the extension's built-in engine) swaps each sensitive value for a placeholder, such as EMAIL_1 or PHONE_1.
3. The AI service gets the message with placeholders only.
4. In the reply, Veil swaps the placeholders back for your real values.

Redaction runs on your computer. Your real values are not sent to the AI service, and they are not sent to us.

WHAT VEIL HIDES
• Email addresses and phone numbers
• Card numbers and bank accounts (IBANs), validated to keep false alarms low
• API keys, tokens and passwords
• IP addresses and web addresses
• National ID numbers
• Names of people, companies and places, with optional name detection that runs on your computer
• Your own words: client names, project codenames, internal terms and custom rules

ATTACHED DOCUMENTS
With the Veil app, PDFs and text files you attach (TXT, Markdown, CSV, JSON, code and similar) are redacted the same way before the site uploads them. The site gets their text, with the sensitive values replaced.

NOTHING SLIPS THROUGH
If neither the Veil app nor the built-in engine can protect a message, it waits instead of going out unprotected, and a notice says why. You can turn this off per site if you prefer.

AT A GLANCE
The toolbar button shows whether the current site is protected and how many values were hidden in the tab.

FREE BUILT-IN ENGINE, OR THE VEIL APP
The extension works on its own, for free: its built-in engine hides emails, phone numbers, cards, IBANs, API keys, IP addresses, national IDs and your own terms. The Veil app for Mac, Windows or Linux adds attached documents, name detection and protection for desktop apps and developer tools; when it is running, the extension uses it. Supported sites: claude.ai and chatgpt.com.

PRIVACY
What you type goes only to the Veil app on your computer, or stays in the extension's own engine. The extension's only request of its own reads a version file from getveil.tech, to tell you when a newer Veil is out. It has no analytics or tracking, and it does not collect or sell your data.
